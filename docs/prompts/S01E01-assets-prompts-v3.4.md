## How decors and styles combine

* The scene content of a decor is style independent : one English scene description per decor, written once (section 4).
* The rendering is style dependent : pick the matching DECOR OPENER (section 1), add the PERIOD TREATMENT line when the shot is in the ÉPOQUE register, then paste the scene description, then the decor Avoid base. D3 and D33 are CADRE register : no period treatment, ever.
* A plate generated in one style is never reused in another style : switching style means regenerating every plate.
* Period characters change with the style : in Styles A and C they are cartoon characters in muted earth tones ; in Style B everyone is an inkman, heroes and period figures alike, with the locked mitten hand rule. The heroes stay identifiable through their reserved colors and totems (sand jacket and hat, teal sweatshirt, orange cap) while period inkmen stay in muted earth tones with era markers ; the richly painted backgrounds carry all the atmosphere. All 33 character combinations are written in full in section 2.
